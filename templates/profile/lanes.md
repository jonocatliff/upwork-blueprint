# Default lanes

The five lanes `/context` offers every member first. A member ticks every service they can picture doing, and `/context` narrows that to two or three. The course named under each lane is the lesson in the community classroom to watch before the first application. A direction that fits none of these is a custom addition, offered after the lanes.

## 1. GoHighLevel CRM automations

Course: the sales automation and GoHighLevel course.
Profile orientation: `gohighlevel.md`.

- CRM build and clean-up: pipelines, custom fields, tags
- Lead capture: forms, funnels, booking calendars
- Follow-up sequences by email and text message
- Missed-call text-back and review requests
- Reporting dashboards for the owner
- Migration from another CRM

## 2. Website building

Course: the CRO course.
Profile orientation: `wordpress.md`.

- Business websites on WordPress
- Landing pages built to convert
- Redesign of an existing site
- Speed and mobile fixes
- Contact forms, booking and payment set-up

## 3. SEO

Course: the SEO course.
Profile orientation: `seo-cro.md`.

- Technical SEO audit and fixes
- Local SEO and Google Business Profile
- On-page optimization and service pages
- Keyword research and content plan
- Monthly reporting

## 4. Google Ads

Course: the Google Ads course and the CRO course.
Profile orientation: `google-ads-cro.md`.

- Search campaign set-up
- Account audit and clean-up
- Conversion tracking set-up
- Landing page testing
- Monthly optimization and reporting

## 5. Automations

Course: the Claude Code Masterclass.
Profile orientation: none yet.

- Workflow automation with Make or n8n
- Tools and scripts built with Claude Code
- AI assistants and chatbots on a business's own data
- Lead, invoice and document processing
- Data syncing between tools
- Voice agents for inbound calls

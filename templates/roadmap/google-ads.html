What the audit finds

  • Whether your conversion tracking is telling the truth, which decides whether any other number in the account means anything.
  • Calls counted, or not counted at all. On a business where the phone carries half the leads, that is usually the largest number in the report.
  • Where the money goes that never comes back: search terms, spend outside your area, and networks that should not be on a search campaign.
  • Proven waste kept separate from blind spend. No headline percentage of your budget, because that figure has no source worth quoting.

What it costs you

  • Nothing. No fee, no contract, no obligation of any kind.
  • You get the report as a page you can open and keep, whatever you decide afterwards.
  • You get the findings ranked by money, each one with the evidence behind it.
  • If it reads wrong, you have lost an afternoon of mine and none of yours.

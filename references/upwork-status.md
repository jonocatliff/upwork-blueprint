# Score and badges: how Upwork judges a freelancer

The Job Success Score and the three badges, from Upwork's own help articles, read
26 September 2026 and cited by article number under
`support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/`. Those pages carry no date of their own.
Nothing here is a lever a command can pull; it is what a member is measured by, so
a command quotes it accurately or stays quiet.

## Job Success Score

Recalculated daily over three windows, 6, 12 and 24 months, and **the best of the
three counts** (211068358, 38437458199059). The published formula: successful
contract outcomes minus negative outcomes, divided by total outcomes. It appears
once there are at least two outcomes in 24 months with at least two clients
(38437546570643), and the clock runs from the **last transaction**, not the end of
the contract (38437480577939).

What feeds it (32389629156755):

- Client satisfaction, and **private feedback counts**, which may differ from the
  public review.
- Long contracts weigh more: payment beyond 90 days counts as a successful outcome
  even without feedback, and each 90 days counts as an additional job, up to a
  weight of eight.
- Higher earnings on a contract weigh more.

Ineligible outcomes: older than 24 months, no earnings or a cancellation before
payment with no feedback, no client feedback at all, or a client who broke Upwork's
policies.

What lowers it (38440123106195, 38439816969875): negative feedback, a cancellation
without payment, disputes, losing long-term clients, poor feedback on a
well-paid contract. No payment **and** no feedback has no effect; no payment **with**
negative feedback does. Above 90% is called excellent; below 79% Upwork says a
member "may find it difficult to connect with new clients".

Feedback is removed only for a terms violation, within seven business days, one
report per feedback (219801228). The old Top Rated perk that let a member erase one
bad review is no longer documented there.

**Not documented:** the exact weighting, and what a given star rating is worth.

## The badges, reviewed every two weeks

Upwork gates paid consultations behind a badge and names **Expert-Vetted** there as a
fourth one beyond the three below. This Blueprint has measured nothing about it, so
it is named and not described.

**Rising Talent.** An invitation, or an average of 4.8 stars plus at least $250
earned in twelve months. On top: a 100% complete profile, a proposal or work inside
90 days, a JSS of 90% or better if one exists, no account holds, **no negative
public or private feedback**, a verified identity, a valid payout method, and good
standing. The benefit is 30 Connects (360049702614).

**Top Rated**, the top 10%. JSS at 90% or better, a first project more than 90 days
ago, Rising Talent or a JSS of 90% in 13 of the last 16 weeks, a 100% complete
profile, at least $1,000 earned in twelve months, current availability, good
standing, and activity inside 90 days (211068468).

**Top Rated Plus**, the top 3%. Top Rated held, more than $10,000 earned in twelve
months, and at least one large contract in twelve months with earnings in it and no
negative outcome. "Large" depends on the category: $5,000 in design, engineering
and architecture, legal, translation and writing, $15,000 in web, mobile and
software, $20,000 in customer support, $10,000 everywhere else (360050417233).

**How a badge is lost:** taking payment off the platform, or repeatedly moving
communication off it before a contract exists. The badge goes and cannot be earned
again for six months (360052511133).

## What this means for the Blueprint

A badge is the result of finishing contracts well, not a step in a course, and the
only part a command can act on is the 100% complete profile that two of the three
require. Quote a criterion with its number when the member asks, and never imply a
badge can be worked toward faster than the contracts allow.

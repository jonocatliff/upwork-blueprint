# The profile blueprint: every field, and what belongs in it

What a complete Upwork profile consists of, field by field: what exists, who can
write it, what makes it finished. **Why a field wins is in
[profile-formula.md](profile-formula.md)** (three measured top earners), **how
being found works is in [upwork-visibility.md](upwork-visibility.md)**, and the
scores and badges are in [upwork-status.md](upwork-status.md).

Sources are Upwork's own help articles, read 26 September 2026 and cited by their
article number under `support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/`. Those pages carry no
date of their own. Where a rule here comes from practice rather than Upwork, the
line says so.

Specialized profiles no longer exist: removed 28 May 2026, their titles, overviews
and skills deleted for good, and Upwork states rankings did not change (115013750068).
One profile, one direction.

## One principle per area, decided

The rule that settles a trade-off in each part of the profile. The evidence for each
one is in the sections below, in [profile-language.md](profile-language.md) from
sixteen real profiles, or in [profile-formula.md](profile-formula.md) from three
measured top earners.

1. **Title:** every block is a word a client types into search, because the title is
   its own search field. A pipe holds keywords, never a slogan.
2. **Overview opening:** the first 250 characters decide alone, because Upwork hides
   the rest. They belong to the client's problem, not to a biography.
3. **Proof placement:** the strongest verifiable proof comes first, and a badge or a
   client count named in prose is not proof. What the aggregate shows is.
4. **Skills:** the client's vocabulary in Upwork's own spelling, counted from real
   postings, every slot filled. Never a guessed name.
5. **Employment history:** what that employer's customer got, not what the role was
   called. Work outside freelancing belongs in it, and it buys no reputation on the
   platform, only a reader's attention.
6. **Portfolio:** four items, because four is where completeness caps out. A number
   in a title only when the proof file ties that number to that project.
7. **Rate:** a band from the measured discipline and the experience level a client
   would pick, moved by verified proof and nothing else. Never a country discount, and
   never empty, because clients filter on it.
8. **Filter fields:** category, languages, English level, timezone, availability. Each
   one is a gate a client can close, so an empty field fails silently rather than
   looking untidy.
9. **Photo and video:** the photo is a face, because it is one of four things a client
   sees before opening anything. The video is optional and last: worth 10% of
   completeness and no measured views.
10. **The close:** one imperative plus permission, of the shape "send me what you have,
    even if it is messy". Never a close about yourself.
11. **Every claim carries its own evidence**, or it does not get written. A missing
    number stays missing; an invented one ends at the first client call.

## The completeness recipe, documented

Completeness is the only ranking lever Upwork admits to, and 100% is a
precondition for Rising Talent and Top Rated. The percentages are published
(211063188):

**The mandatory half.** Photo, overview, at least one employment history entry,
at least one skill tag. Together 50%.

**The optional half.** Portfolio 5% each up to 20%. Further employment entries 10%
each up to 20%. Further skill tags 10%. Education 10% each up to 20%. Profile
video 10%. At least one linked account 10%. Certification 5% each up to 10%. At
least one other experience 5%.

A beginner reaches 100% without a single Upwork contract: four portfolio items, two
employment entries, two education entries, a linked account, one other experience,
and the video. **What is documented is the completeness percentage, not a ranking
effect per field.** Upwork says a complete profile ranks better; it never says the
video or a certificate does. Fill them because a buyer uses them, and because 100% is
a precondition for Rising Talent and Top Rated.

## Appear where the strong profiles appear

The goal of the title, the skills and the category together is one thing: turn up in
the same searches as the strong profiles in the chosen direction. That is now
measurable from two sides, and the overlap is the answer.

**From the demand side**, count the `skills` arrays of current postings in the lane.
**From the supply side**, read the skills of profiles in that lane that carry a badge
or real earnings. The names that appear on both lists are the set to fill first,
because they are what clients type and what the strong profiles already hold.

From the sixteen profiles read on 26 September 2026, the recurring sets were: HighLevel,
CRM Automation, Marketing Automation, Sales Funnel, Lead Generation and Email Automation
for GoHighLevel; n8n, Make.com, Zapier, API Integration and AI Agent Development for
automation; AI Agent Development, AI Chatbot, Python, LangChain, RAG and OpenAI API for
chatbots; WordPress Development, Elementor, WooCommerce, Page Speed Optimization and
Website Redesign for WordPress. All sixteen fill every skill slot they have.

Being in those searches is the part a member controls on day one. The badge and the
earnings are not, which is why the words come first.

## Where the keywords come from, measured rather than guessed

Two fields decide whether a profile turns up at all: the title, because clients can
search titles alone, and the skills, because they are ANDed filter fields. So the
question is not which words sound professional but which words clients actually type.

**Harvest them from real postings.** Every job `find_jobs` returns carries Upwork's
own `skills` array, which is the client's vocabulary in Upwork's exact spelling. Pull
twenty to thirty current postings in the member's lane, count how often each skill
name appears, and the top of that list is the skill set and the title vocabulary.
Nothing to invent, and it can be repeated any month.

From the ten newest GoHighLevel postings on 26 September 2026, the count ran: CRM
Automation and Marketing Automation four times each, HighLevel and Sales Funnel three
times each, then Lead Generation, Social Media Marketing, Conversational AI, AI
Chatbot, Email Marketing, ManyChat. Note that **the skill is called HighLevel, not
GoHighLevel**, which is exactly the kind of detail a guessed skill list gets wrong.

**Three kinds of words to harvest**, from
[upwork-job-search.md](upwork-job-search.md): the tools the client runs, the role
titles they hire for, and the problem words they use when they do not know the
solution. A title built only from the last kind competes with everyone; one built from
the first two turns up in searches with fewer freelancers in them.

**Then the keyword line.** The measured top profiles carry a block near the end of the
overview that exists for search rather than for humans, one of them headed openly as
keywords a human can skip. It costs a line and covers the spellings the prose never
uses.

## The fields the connector reads and can write

Title, overview and skills writes are documented as previews and untested, so a
paste version always comes with them.

**1. Title.** The line under the name, and its own search field: clients can
restrict a search to titles alone (1500007918681), so a title without the words a
client types drops out of that search. **Upwork documents no character limit**; the
70-character target and the three or four blocks split by `|` come from practice
and from the measured profiles, not from Upwork. Finished when every block is a
service, tool or audience, and none is a benefit phrase.

**2. Overview.** Upwork states that **only the first 250 or so characters appear in
the search results list** (360016252373), which is why the opening carries the
decision: an outcome, or a hard number when one is verified. Structure comes from
Unicode bold or emoji, because Upwork strips markdown. No documented length limit.
Finished when it opens with what the client gets, names the tools once for search,
and ends with a specific ask.

**3. Skills.** ANDed filter fields in client search, and Upwork contradicts itself
on how many: "up to 15 skills" (360016252373) against "up to 20 skills to your
profile" (211060318). The connector's `set_skills` documents 20, and two of three
measured top profiles carry 20. Fill every slot the profile offers, read the real cap in the live
editor, and claim no number from memory. Soft-skill tags are separate, generated by Upwork from public reviews, not
editable, and do not affect the Job Success Score (45958886350099).

**4. Availability.** Hours per week and whether the member is open to offers.
Finished when it matches the hours they can really answer in.

**5. Employment history.** Up to 100 entries. Every relevant role with employer,
title and period, including work outside freelancing. Worth 10% of completeness
each up to 20%, so two entries are free percentage points. Finished when each entry
says what the client of that employer got, not what the job was called.

**6. Education.** 10% each up to 20%. Optional, never padded.

**7. Languages.** Each with its proficiency, plus the English level Upwork asks
for separately. Finished when it is honest: a client who books a call on "fluent"
and meets "conversational" is a refund.

## The fields that stay manual, so the command hands over text and a click path

**8. Hourly rate.** The connector cannot set it, and Upwork says the rate "helps
clients filter search results" (360016252373). It anchors every later bid, so
`context/me.md` carries it and `/pitch-page` reads it there.

**9. Portfolio.** Documented limits: title up to 70 characters, role up to 100,
description up to 600, up to 5 skill tags, images recommended at 1000 by 750 and at
least 400 by 300 (360016144974). Linking a real Upwork job notifies that client,
who has three days to object. **No contact details in the files or on any page they
link to.** Each item is 5% of completeness up to 20%, so four items are the
efficient number. A title carries a number only when the proof file ties it to that
project.

**10. Intro video.** A YouTube link with monetisation off. Upwork documents no
length limit; 30 to 90 seconds is practice. Worth 10% of completeness, which is the
only documented reason to make one. The "30% more views" figure that circulates has no
reachable source.

**11. Profile photo.** A real portrait. Sunglasses, logos, clipart, group photos
and edited images are not allowed (360016252373).

**12. Location and timezone.** Clients filter by both, including a US-only filter.
A wrong timezone makes every reply look late.

**13. Certificates.** 5% each up to 10%. Only what can be checked. Tier C in the
formula: never the lead. Upwork's own Skill Certifications are discontinued, and of
the 40 newest postings in our four lanes exactly one mentioned a certificate at all.
The one with a documented return is **Partner Certified Talent**: 150 free Connects
per partner certification, and Upwork says it feeds search and matching. Details and
prices per lane in [upwork-measure.md](upwork-measure.md).

**14. Other experiences.** Up to 100 entries, 5% of completeness for the first.
The place for volunteer work, a side project or a system built at a non-freelance
job. Nobody in the measured sample uses it, which makes it free space for a career
changer.

**15. Categories.** Upwork's own articles disagree again: "up to four categories …
Your profile will be displayed in these categories when clients search through
them" (360016252373) against up to 10 elsewhere. Pick the ones that match the
direction from `/context`, not the member's old job. The expensive mistake is a
boost, which targets category plus specialty.

**16. Linked accounts.** 10% of completeness for the first one, and the cheapest
percentage point on the list.

**17. Testimonials.** **Upwork no longer accepts new testimonial requests**
(1500002004322). Existing ones stay, can be shown or hidden, cannot be edited, and
do not affect the Job Success Score. Any guide telling a member to collect them is
out of date.

## What Upwork forbids in a profile

- **No means of direct contact anywhere in it:** phone, email, address, a link to a
  contact form, an external application system, social handles. User Agreement 7.2,
  version 8.2 effective 20 July 2026, calls a breach material and permanent
  suspension possible.
- **One account and one freelancer profile** without written permission (1.4).
- **Nothing accurate left out and nothing invented** about identity, location,
  business, skills or services (1.3).
- **No other people's work in the portfolio**, no advertising for services outside
  Upwork, no personal contact details (360016252373).
- **No invented relationship** to a company or person, and no subcontracting
  without the client's consent (9127142196243).
- Documented absence: **no rule against a guarantee or a money-back promise** in a
  profile. Checked the full agreement and the profile policy pages. This Blueprint
  still avoids them, because a promise the member cannot keep costs the contract,
  not because Upwork bans them.

## When a profile counts as complete

Every field above either carries content or a named reason why it stays empty, and
the completeness percentage on the Find Work page reads 100. A first profile is
allowed to be thin in portfolio, video and testimonials, and strong in title,
overview, skills and employment history, which are the four a beginner can fill
fully on day one.

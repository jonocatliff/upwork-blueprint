# How to decide: the working rules

The durable part. Prices, limits, rankings and demand belong in the dated files
next door ([upwork-market.md](upwork-market.md),
[upwork-visibility.md](upwork-visibility.md), [upwork-status.md](upwork-status.md),
[upwork-facts.json](upwork-facts.json), which `python3 code/facts.py` reads:
`get <id>` for one number with its source and the date it was read, `topic <name>`
for a field, `stale --days 120` for what is old enough to re-check); this file
holds the methods that survive
Upwork changing them. Adapted 26 September 2026 from a Maker School research pass
whose full evidence trail, 79 source records, stays outside the shipped repo.

**Evidence order.** The member's own verified project evidence supports claims about
the member. Current Upwork policy governs conduct on the platform, and the account's
live interface shows what that account can actually do. Where those disagree, verify
with Upwork before relying on a feature. Community advice and other members'
outcomes are ideas to test. A market report describes its own sample and period,
nothing more. A newer source does not prove a tactic works for this account.

## The ten rules

**D01 · Pick the buyer before the words.** One target buyer, one recurring problem,
one service outcome, decided before the title exists. Name a tool only when it
explains a real deliverable.

**D02 · Open with something verifiable.** The most relevant provable result, or a
precise offer. The first sentence has to work alone. Inspect the real profile
preview before publishing instead of designing around a remembered character limit.

**D03 · One shape for every case.** Starting problem, the member's own role, the
delivered system, the documented outcome, the period. Clients are named only with
permission. A self-built demo is labelled a demo, and somebody else's work or an
estimated revenue figure is never presented as proven.

**D04 · Fill fields because a buyer uses them.** Portfolio, skills, credentials,
employment and availability stay accurate and relevant. Completeness is worth
having on its own terms, not because an unmeasured ranking claim promises a lift.

**D05 · Answer the posting, not the category.** Each proposal addresses the client's
stated problem. Offer a process map, prototype or walkthrough only when it exists,
say what is unfinished, and keep everything before the contract inside Upwork's
current rules.

**D06 · Paid features are experiments, one at a time.** Proposal boosts, profile
boosts, badges and subscriptions each get their own spend limit, derived from the
total possible cost and the qualified replies or hires actually observed. No other
member's bid, budget or return transfers to this account.

**D07 · Measure the funnel, change one thing.** Record job fit, applications, total
spend, views, qualified replies and hires. Change one variable at a time, targeting
or opening or spend, and compare a stricter filter against a broader one with the
member's own numbers.

**D08 · Price from the work, not from the market's ceiling.** Scope, delivery cost,
verified proof and how buyers respond set the price. A posted job maximum and
another freelancer's rate are examples, never a pricing rule.

**D09 · Read market reports for problems, not forecasts.** They point at buyer
problems and examples worth investigating. A dated sample, a tool mention or a
hand-picked premium posting is not a demand forecast.

**D10 · Only what the connector actually exposes.** Use the actions the official
Upwork MCP offers this account, confirm every write before it runs, and check
current rules before any scheduled, hosted or stored-data workflow. Old scraper and
API anecdotes do not describe the official connector.

## What has to be on the table before a profile is drafted

**Buyer and offer:** target industry, buyer role, problem, service outcome, and the
tools the member genuinely uses. **Proof:** two or three comparable finished
projects with role, deliverable, result, period and where the evidence lives, with
missing metrics marked unknown. **Portfolio:** a link or artifact per case, plus
permission to name a client or show material, and self-built work marked as a demo.
**Commercial fit:** scope, availability, delivery cost, and a rate rationale built
from the member's own work.

**Draft order:** title, then the first sentence, then two or three short case cards,
then services and process, then the portfolio and the factual fields. A case card
reads: context, problem, our role, deliverable, documented outcome and period,
evidence and permission. An unmeasured outcome is described by what the system does,
without inventing a number, and a placeholder is never published as a claim.

## Check these in the live account, not from memory

Everything here can change, so no remembered number or feature state is a standing
rule.

**V01** the profile preview, the available skill tags and the editor's real limits,
before publishing or rewriting the profile. **V02** eligibility and price for
consultations, badges, boosts and subscriptions, before enabling or paying for one.
**V03** the base and possible extra cost of a proposal, refund conditions, and
whether a submitted proposal can still be boosted, before budgeting or boosting.
**V04** the freelancer fee shown for this actual proposal or offer, before quoting
net income or accepting work. **V05** the current rules on pre-contract
communication and file sharing, before moving a conversation or sharing any contact
or booking detail. **V06** which MCP actions this account is given, and the current
policy for the exact automation, storage or schedule proposed, before a write or a
persistent workflow. **V07** a fresh, methodologically comparable market source,
before changing specialization or pricing because of a trend.

**Verification state:** none of V01 to V07 has been checked inside a live account
for this file. The dated sources were read on 26 September 2026, which is not the
same thing. When a check happens, record its date, the account it ran in and the
evidence next to the decision it changes, and drop the superseded value from the
working layer while leaving the dated note intact.
